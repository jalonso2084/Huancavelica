Here is the translated information about the potato varieties INIA-303 Canchan, INIA-302 Amarilis, and Yungay, based on the document Identification of Elite Potato Clones with Resistance to Late Blight Through Participatory Varietal Selection in Peru.

1. Disease Resistance (Especially to Late Blight)
INIA-303 Canchan: Susceptible to Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans).
INIA-302 Amarilis: Moderately resistant to Late Blight.
Yungay: Susceptible to Late Blight.
The document highlights that these varieties are among the most widely grown in Peru, but their high susceptibility to Late Blight has driven the need to develop clones with greater resistance to the disease.

2. Yield Potential
INIA-303 Canchan: In trials, it yielded 33.61 t/ha in Cañaypata and 20.94 t/ha in Arcopampa, making it a high-yielding variety under certain conditions.
INIA-302 Amarilis: Showed variable yields, reaching up to 45.86 t/ha in Macullida and 37.05 t/ha in Chucmar, but with less stability in areas affected by Late Blight.
Yungay: Yields were highly affected by Late Blight in areas with severe disease pressure, dropping to 5.00 t/ha in Chucmar, although under better conditions, it achieved 49.41 t/ha in Macullida.

3. Adaptability to Climate and Soil Conditions
INIA-303 Canchan and Yungay: Selected by farmers for altitudes above 3500 masl, due to their adaptation to cold climates and Andean soils.
INIA-302 Amarilis: Performed better in locations with lower Late Blight pressure and well-drained soils.
Yungay: Poor adaptability in areas with high humidity and Late Blight pressure, but good yields in dry climates.

4. Susceptibility to Environmental Stresses (Drought, Frost, Pests)
INIA-303 Canchan and Yungay: Highly susceptible to Late Blight, with reduced yields in areas with high humidity and continuous rainfall.
INIA-302 Amarilis: Greater resistance in environments with biotic and abiotic stress, but variable yield stability depending on location.
Yungay: Does not tolerate frost or drought well, which affects its stability in extreme altitudes.

5. Market Value and Farmer Preferences
INIA-303 Canchan and Yungay: Among the most widely consumed varieties in Peru, covering more than 70% of the cultivated area.
INIA-303 Canchan: Preferred for its color, texture, and yield, although farmers are looking for more disease-resistant alternatives.
INIA-302 Amarilis: Its moderate resistance to Late Blight makes it attractive in areas with high disease pressure, but its market adoption remains lower compared to Yungay.

Conclusion
The varieties INIA-303 Canchan and Yungay are highly susceptible to Late Blight, limiting their yield stability in humid conditions. While they are very popular in the market, farmers have shown interest in more resistant varieties that can maintain stable yields without heavy reliance on fungicides. In contrast, INIA-302 Amarilis offers better resistance, but its market adoption remains lower.
